Mine and Refine: Optimizing Graded Relevance in E-commerce Semantic Search Retrieval 10
OpenAlex · paper · 6 Nov · arXiv · PDF · OpenAlex
🏢 DoorDash · 4 citations
Embedding-based retrieval (EBR) for large-scale e-commerce search faces three intertwined challenges: graded (non-binary) relevance where engagement signals are noisy and intent-varying while business relevance guidelines admit acceptable-but-not-exact matches, false negatives in hard sample mining, and unstable similarity score separability across relevance levels, the last of which complicates hybrid search score fusion and downstream ranking.

SAM-D2Q: Aligning Multimodal Doc2Query with Search Demand and Conversion for E-commerce 10
OpenAlex · paper · 6 Nov · arXiv · PDF · OpenAlex
🏢 Alibaba
The paper introduces SAM-D2Q, a business-aligned multimodal document expansion framework designed to fix vocabulary mismatches in e-commerce search. Traditional text-only Doc2Query methods often miss visual product attributes and produce commercially ineffective expansions. SAM-D2Q integrates search demand, conversion objectives, and product imagery to generate better pseudo-queries.
Why it matters: Directly targets Search & query understanding by bridging the gap between short user queries, visual fashion attributes, and commercial conversion goals.
