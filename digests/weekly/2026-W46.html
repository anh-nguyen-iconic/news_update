🏆 Top 10 of the week
 
Generative Search and Recommendation at Shopify 10
arXiv · paper · 9 Nov · arXiv · PDF
Search and recommendation at Shopify require retrieving relevant products from a changing catalog of billions of products under strict latency constraints. We present SID-LLM, which casts both tasks as generating semantic IDs: short sequences of content-derived tokens representing products. We jointly pre-train SID-LLM on catalog and behavioral data, giving search and recommendation a shared representation and training objective.

Hybrid Product Search Without the Copy: Lexical, Semantic and Filtered Retrieval Served Directly from a Data Lake 10
arXiv · paper · 10 Nov · arXiv · PDF
E-commerce catalogs increasingly live in lakehouse tables: Apache Iceberg metadata over Parquet files in object storage. Product search, however, still runs on copies of that catalog, in a lexical engine and a vector database, each with its own ingestion pipeline, schema, staleness window and bill. We show that search applications can be hosted directly on the data lake, with the lakehouse table as the single system of record.

Semantic profile construction for explainable and semantically consistent multimodal fashion recommendation 10
OpenAlex · paper · 7 Nov · DOI · OpenAlex
San Jose State University
Fashion recommendation requires heterogeneous visual and textual product content to be transformed into representations that are both retrievable and interpretable. We present FACTOR (Feature-Aware Contextual Optimization and Refinement), a framework that constructs six-field semantic profiles comprising category, subcategory, color, material, pattern, and cross-dataset style information, and combines them with image and multimodal retrieval.

OpenAlex · paper · 3 Nov · DOI · OpenAlex
🏢 Alibaba
E-commerce search often suffers from vocabulary mismatch between user queries and merchant-authored product titles, since short titles cannot fully cover diverse user expressions or visual product attributes. Although Doc2Query alleviates this issue by generating pseudo-queries for document expansion, traditional methods are text-only and not optimized for e-commerce business objectives.

Mine and Refine: Optimizing Graded Relevance in E-commerce Semantic Search Retrieval 10
OpenAlex · paper · 3 Nov · DOI · OpenAlex
🏢 DoorDash · 2 citations
Embedding-based retrieval (EBR) for large-scale e-commerce search faces three intertwined challenges: graded (non-binary) relevance where engagement signals are noisy and intent-varying while business relevance guidelines admit acceptable-but-not-exact matches, false negatives in hard sample mining, and unstable similarity score separability across relevance levels, the last of which complicates hybrid search score fusion and downstream ranking.

Deterministic Entity Resolution: Schema-Derived Decision Logic for Variant Disambiguation and Precision Queries in Retrieval-Augmented Commerce Search 10
OpenAlex · paper · 23 Oct · DOI · OpenAlex
Independent Researcher Cumming , GA , USA
This study introduces schema-derived decision logic to resolve product variants deterministically in retrieval-augmented commerce search. Using 171 instances across six query patterns, the method leverages attributes like brand, model terms, and query intent markers to disambiguate items prior to ranking.
