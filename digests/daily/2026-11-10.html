Generative Search and Recommendation at Shopify

21h ago

Shopify presents SID-LLM, a generative model that unifies product search and recommendation by representing billions of catalog items as short semantic IDs (SIDs). The model uses residual k-means tokenization to encode product embeddings into discrete tokens, which are then generated using either autoregressive decoding or multi-token prediction (MTP) conditioned on catalog and behavioral data. To balance latency and quality, the production search stack pairs MTP with a lightweight ranker, while recommendations use an autoregressive model with joint-probability product resolution. Online A/B tests demonstrate significant gains in conversion rates, and offline evaluations show SID-LLM outperforms existing candidate generators.

Key ideas
1 Represent products as short semantic IDs (SIDs) using residual k-means (RQ-KMeans) quantization with 8192-8192-8192 codebooks, grouping items with shared search intent.
2 Use multi-token prediction (MTP) combined with a lightweight ranker for search retrieval to cut median latency in half while maintaining high relevance scores.
3 Pre-train on a mixed data stream comprising product attribute data, search interaction sessions, LLM-judged relevance examples, and historical recommendation sequences.
4 Employ autoregressive models with joint-probability product resolution and discovery streams for recommendations to maximize incremental pool recall.
📊 Results
SID-LLM achieved an incremental pool recall of 2.65 points (61% higher than the second-best generator) in offline recommendation evaluations, and an online A/B test on the home feed raised attributed orders by 7.95% and conversion rate by 9.85%.
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Implement residual k-means semantic ID (SID) tokenization on THE ICONIC product catalog to map fashion items into discrete semantic tokens for search and recommendation.
  • Adopt multi-token prediction combined with a lightweight ranker to speed up vector retrieval latency for high-traffic fashion search queries.
  • Incorporate discovery streams and interaction sequence compression (dropping old clicks while retaining purchases) into personalization and recommendation pipelines to improve discovery of novel products.

Semantic profile construction for explainable and semantically consistent multimodal fashion recommendation

7 Nov · San Jose State University

Fashion recommendation systems need to convert diverse visual and textual data into representations that are both retrievable and interpretable. This paper introduces FACTOR, a framework that builds six-field semantic profiles including category, subcategory, color, material, pattern, and style information. It combines these profiles with image and multimodal retrieval using hierarchy-constrained decoding, cosine-based semantic retrieval, and late fusion. The approach is evaluated through both consistency metrics and human judgments. Results demonstrate high accuracy across various attribute classification and retrieval consistency tasks.

Key ideas
1 Construct six-field semantic profiles covering category, subcategory, color, material, pattern, and cross-dataset style information to improve explainability.
2 Integrate hierarchy-constrained category-subcategory decoding to maintain logical consistency in product classification.
3 Combine profile-derived semantic retrieval with image and multimodal retrieval using a late fusion strategy.
4 Utilize LLaMA 3 for automated metadata extraction to populate semantic profiles.
📊 Results
FACTOR achieved a category accuracy of 0.93, subcategory accuracy of 0.72, and semantic similarity of 0.85-0.87 for color, material, and pattern. In a 125-image in-domain audit, it reached category/subcategory accuracy of 0.78/0.50 and multi-label F1 scores of 0.39, 0.22, and 0.28 for color, material, and pattern, while LLaMA 3 metadata extraction achieved 0.64 precision.
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Adopt the six-field semantic profile structure to enrich product metadata for improved search and recommendation explainability.
  • Explore hierarchy-constrained decoding for category and subcategory classification to ensure logical consistency in catalog tagging.
  • Evaluate LLaMA 3 for automated attribute extraction from product images and text to scale catalog enrichment.

Where Do LLMs Add Value in E-Commerce Shopping Advisors? A Stage-Based Comparison of Retrieval, Reranking, and Evidence Augmentation

15 Oct · University of Science and Technology Beijing

E-commerce shopping advisors struggle with short queries and attribute constraints, prompting questions about where Large Language Models add the most value in the search pipeline. This paper evaluates BM25, dense retrieval, LLM reranking, and LLM retrieval followed by reranking using an aligned benchmark of Amazon data. The authors conduct a stage-based system comparison across 10,961 queries to determine whether LLMs are better suited for initial candidate retrieval or subsequent reranking. The main findings indicate that LLMs add the most value as fine-grained rerankers rather than expanding retrieval coverage, significantly boosting ranking metrics like NDCG while yielding marginal gains when used for retrieval.

Key ideas
1 Deploy LLMs primarily as rerankers rather than retrievers in e-commerce search pipelines to maximize ranking quality without wasting compute on broad retrieval.
2 Use LLMs specifically for product-type disambiguation and fine-grained attribute matching where short user queries lack detail.
3 Avoid relying on unselective review-augmented evidence during reranking, as metadata-only reranking outperformed review-augmented reranking in their study.
4 Ensure the initial candidate generation stage is strong, as LLM reranking fails when the initial candidate set is weak.
📊 Results
Across 10,961 benchmark queries using Amazon Shopping Queries and Reviews 2023 Electronics metadata, LLM reranking increased NDCG@5 from 0.922099 (dense retrieval) to 0.960640 while maintaining identical Hit@5 and Recall@5. Extending the LLM to retrieval yielded only a marginal Recall@5 increase from 0.696785 to 0.700000 and NDCG@5 from 0.960640 to 0.961210.
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Refactor THE ICONIC product search architecture to keep traditional dense retrieval for candidate generation and apply an LLM reranker to optimize top-K ranking and NDCG.
  • Leverage LLM reranking models to better handle ambiguous, short fashion queries and fine-grained attribute matching (e.g., specific cuts, materials, or styles).
  • Audit current generative AI or review-summarisation features in product discovery to ensure evidence augmentation like customer reviews is tightly query-aligned and does not degrade ranking quality.

microsoft/TokenEconomizer

13 Oct · ⭐ 5.4k (+430 today, +1k this week)

TokenEconomizer is an experience-driven, black-box cost control plugin for coding agents that reduces operational expenses without modifying the underlying agent. It analyzes completed sessions within a specific repository to cut waste through three distinct mechanisms: micro-skills, tool pruning, and failure-loop intervention. The system dynamically learns repository-specific facts, eliminates unused tool definitions from model context windows, and detects when an agent gets stuck in a loop to provide targeted redirects. Evaluated across multiple agent-model combinations, it successfully lowers average execution costs while maintaining or improving task success rates.

Key ideas
1 Micro-skills mechanism synthesizes repository facts from recurring failures and periodic model reviews every k sessions, injecting valid facts at session start.
2 Tool pruning removes unused tool definitions from request payloads based on prompts and sessions thresholds, dropping inactive tools through native agent settings.
3 Failure-loop intervention uses a judge model to monitor churning turns and provides context redirects when confidence exceeds a set threshold.
4 Cross-session learning state is maintained locally in ~/.tokeneconomizer per repository, ensuring zero manual configuration for credential handling or API keys.
📊 Results
The evaluation on 277 SWE-Bench tasks showed 18.4% to 30.9% lower mean cost across five agent-model combinations, with costs reduced in all 30 repository-combination cells and Pass@1 improved in 4 out of 5 configurations.
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Apply tool pruning concepts to limit unused tool or schema definitions in ecommerce LLM shopping agents and internal retrieval systems, reducing token payload costs.
  • Implement failure-loop intervention logic to detect when ranking or recommendation orchestration agents get stuck in repetitive error loops during complex user queries.
  • Use cross-session experience accumulation to inject environment facts and catalog conventions at the start of automated code generation or data science pipeline tasks.

Useful Diversity or Hallucinated Variety? Persona-Conditioned Synthetic Query Generation for E-Commerce Search

26 Oct · Manchester Metropolitan University

Training robust e-commerce semantic parsers requires extensive labelled data, making synthetic query generation a scalable solution. However, increasing prompt diversity often conflicts with schema faithfulness, introducing hallucinations or dropped constraints. This paper evaluates 118,098 synthetic query pairs produced by 6 LLMs using 9 persona-conditioned prompt families on a UK automotive schema, comparing them against 1,214 human-annotated and 15,304 production queries. The authors find that the IPIP personality bundle consistently improves parser F1 across all matched contrasts, while syntax conditioning increases real-traffic alignment and diversity at the cost of dropped operator words. Alignment sKL serves as an unlabelled proxy for parser utility when references match, and an LLM-judge audit reveals that dropped constraints dominate universal parser failures.

Key ideas
1 Use IPIP Big Five personality prompts to improve semantic parser F1, yielding consistent gains over vanilla prompts.
2 Incorporate explicit syntax modes (e.g., noun phrases, telegraphic fragments) to drive lexical diversity and align synthetic queries closer to real search traffic, while guarding against dropped operator words.
3 Monitor alignment sKL as an unlabelled proxy for parser utility against matched human reference corpora.
4 Implement a multi-judge LLM committee audit to triage universal parser failures and identify synthetic data hallucinations such as dropped constraints and direction reversals.
📊 Results
The synthetic corpus contains 118,098 query-intent pairs, evaluated against a held-out human reference of 1,214 pairs and 15,304 live production queries. The IPIP personality bundle improves parser F1 by +1.1 to +2.1 points across four matched contrasts (Holm-corrected p < .001).
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Apply IPIP personality-conditioned prompt bundles when generating synthetic training data for THE ICONIC's natural language search and semantic parsing models.
  • Use alignment sKL as an unlabelled quality metric to evaluate synthetic search query distributions against live search traffic logs before fine-tuning ranking or retrieval models.
  • Audit synthetic query pipelines for hallucinated constraints (such as dropped price floors or size filters) using multi-model LLM verification to prevent downstream search retrieval errors.
