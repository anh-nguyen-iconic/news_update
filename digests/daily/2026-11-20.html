ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN E-COMMERCE: EFFECTS ON CUSTOMER SATISFACTION AND LOYALTY

7 Nov · Assistant Professor, Department of Commerce CA , Swami Daya…

Online retailers have rapidly integrated AI into the shopping experience, yet it remains unclear whether these technologies genuinely build long-term customer loyalty or merely complete transactions efficiently. This paper examines three common e-commerce AI touchpoints: personalized product recommendations, conversational customer service chatbots, and real-time dynamic pricing engines. The study investigates how these functions affect consumer perceived satisfaction and downstream loyalty constructs such as repurchase behavior and word-of-mouth advocacy. By analyzing survey responses from active internet shoppers alongside behavioral data on repeated purchases, the research evaluates the distinct impact of each touchpoint. The findings reveal that personalization yields the most significant and consistent positive impact because customers perceive it as a sign of being personally understood rather than just served.

Key ideas
1 Examine the distinct impacts of three core e-commerce AI touchpoints: personalized product recommendations, conversational chatbots, and real-time dynamic pricing engines.
2 Measure consumer perceived satisfaction alongside loyalty constructs including repurchase intent and word-of-mouth advocacy using active shopper surveys and behavioral purchase data.
3 Prioritize AI-driven personalization initiatives as a primary driver of customer satisfaction and brand loyalty, leveraging the insight that tailored experiences make customers feel understood.

From Passive Mining to Proactive Matching: A Survey of AI-Driven User Need Discovery and the Case for Agent-Mediated Demand-Supply Coordination

16 Nov · High Bluff Academy, Rancho Santa Fe, CA, United States

This paper surveys research from 2024 to 2026 on using AI, specifically large language models and LLM-based agents, to discover user needs from user-generated content like product reviews and social media posts. The authors conducted a systematic literature review using Google Scholar with a bilingual query matrix, resulting in a verified corpus of 98 papers after deduplication and screening. The findings show that LLMs dominate current techniques, agent-based approaches are growing, and applications are heavily concentrated in software requirements engineering and e-commerce. The study highlights a shift from passive mining to proactive matching and proposes that application concentration correlates with the volume of public user text available by industry.

Key ideas

2 Apply systematic literature review methodologies using bilingual query matrices and rule-based relevance scoring to map out emerging AI research domains.

mdiri-ai/SoduWork

29 Oct · ⭐ 3.7k (+1.3k today)

SoduWork is an AI-native engine and multi-harness platform designed for teams and their agents to manage workflows from request to sign-off. It addresses the problem of fast AI output paired with slow human trust by integrating explicit acceptance criteria, independent AI reviews, and human sign-offs into a shared issue pool. The engine supports multi-harness runtimes like Pi, OpenCode, and Qwen Code, allowing users to switch models and harnesses mid-conversation while preserving working state. Additionally, successful workflows and prompts can be packaged, reviewed, and shared across teams as versioned plugins. The text outlines self-hosted and desktop deployment options along with governance tools for role-based access and auditing.

Key ideas
1 Define clear acceptance criteria for every AI request, enforcing independent AI review checks before requiring human sign-off.
2 Decouple the agent runtime harness from the model, using a single adapter interface to switch models or harnesses mid-conversation without losing context.
3 Package successful agent workflows, inputs, and rules as versioned plugins that can be reviewed, shared, and managed across teams.
4 Utilize smart dispatch to route routine agent tasks to cheaper or local models while assigning long or complex tasks to dedicated cloud runners.

No quantitative results reported.

DualPath-SSA: Separate Prediction of Strength and Importance on Plural Eccentricity Standards for Explainable and Diverse Outfit Recommendation

1 Nov

This paper introduces DualPath-SSA, a fashion recommendation method designed to provide diverse recommendations based on subjective impressions like eccentricity while maintaining user preference fit. Existing diversification methods typically rely on objective feature differences, failing to control continuous changes in subjective user perceptions. The proposed method quantifies the overall visual eccentricity of an outfit to express a range of subjective impressions, even among outfits sharing a common item. In experiments using human raters, the proposed method successfully widened the eccentricity range significantly compared to traditional similarity-based recommendation approaches. Ultimately, the method matched the performance level of an ideal selection using human ratings and reproduced the population's eccentricity ordering.

Key ideas
1 Quantify overall outfit eccentricity directly from the visual elements composing it to model subjective fashion impressions.
2 Use a dual-path architecture to separate the prediction of strength and importance based on plural eccentricity standards.
3 Control continuous changes in subjective impression independently of objective feature distances in the embedding space.
📊 Results
Tested on 100 outfits sharing a blue shirt and rated by 82 raters, the proposed method widened the human-rated eccentricity range to about twice that of similarity-based recommendation.
🛍 How to apply at THE ICONIC
  • Incorporate subjective impression modeling like visual eccentricity into THE ICONIC's outfit recommendation and homepage styling tiles to enhance diversity beyond simple objective feature distance.
  • Use subjective impression scoring to curate more nuanced 'complete the look' or alternative outfit carousels that maintain user preference fit while varying aesthetic standards.

Enabling Real-Time AI for Scientific Instruments with GPU-Native Data Acquisition

9h ago

Analyzing sensor measurements in real time allows AI to guide experiments and adjust processes, but data movement bottlenecks often limit instrument design. NVIDIA DAQIRI is a software-defined data acquisition library that bridges sensors directly to GPU-ready workflows for analysis and AI inference without writing to disk first. A new collaboration highlights an electron microscopy camera utilizing DAQIRI to process high-dynamic-range data streams exceeding 500 Gbps. The latest DAQIRI release introduces hardware packet reordering via NICs, an optimized ibverbs engine for higher throughput, and direct polling mode for low-latency feedback.

Key ideas
1 Use NVIDIA DAQIRI to move high-bandwidth instrument and sensor data directly into GPU-ready workflows without writing to disk first.
2 Offload packet reordering tasks to supported NVIDIA ConnectX-7 or newer NICs to preserve CPU and GPU resources for analysis.
3 Adopt the ibverbs engine for high-rate receive and transmit of raw Ethernet packets, delivering higher per-core throughput at small packet sizes and extreme data rates.
4 Implement direct polling mode on latency-sensitive TX and RX control queues to let the application thread receive and submit packets directly, reducing communication latency.
📊 Results
The new electron microscopy camera generates more than 500 Gbps, which is over 500x faster than conventional EELS data streams. Application send-to-receive latency on the order of 2-4 microseconds was demonstrated in direct polling mode during cabled loopback benchmark tests.

TAC-CoT is an adaptive implicit Chain-of-Thought framework designed for LLM agents that replaces explicit text rationales with compact latent reasoning segments. This interleaved design enables efficient agentic tool-use without sacrificing task performance.
Why it matters: Useful for reducing latency and token costs in agentic workflows and automated tool use for e-commerce search and query understanding.
